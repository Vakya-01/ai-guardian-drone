Emergency SOS

🚁 What happens after SOS?

1. Your GPS location is immediately sent to the emergency response dashboard.

2. The nearest available police station receives the alert.

3. A Guardian Drone is dispatched toward the location.

4. Police manpower is sent toward the victim's location.

5. If the nearest station is unavailable, the system identifies the next available station.

📱 Automatic Fall Detection

The phone's motion sensors monitor for a possible fall. A possible fall is confirmed only when a sudden movement is followed by a short low-motion period.
